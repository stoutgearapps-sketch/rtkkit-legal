The work

What you can do

  • Layout

    Capture points, build surfaces from those points, square off from a baseline, and set up grade paths for trenches and swales.

  • Plan Import

    Bring in a PDF, image, or KMZ plot plan, tie it to control points, and stake right against the drawing.

  • Cut/fill readout

    A large display shows how far you are above or below target. Set a target by hand, or lock it to a surface or grade path.

    That screen is called Machine Guidance. It shows numbers only and does not control equipment.

  • Site localization

    Line your live position up with the job’s control.

  • Export

    Share points and surfaces as KML, or move a whole job to another device as a project bundle.

  • Feet or meters

    Work in the units the job uses.

How it works

Phone GPS and an RTK receiver

Ready now

Built-in GPS

RTK Kit works with the phone’s built-in GPS right away. A live readout shows the current horizontal and vertical accuracy, so you always know what you’re working with. Phone GPS runs at lower precision than an external RTK receiver, so it is best for rough layout, getting close, and checking progress.

Higher precision

External RTK receiver

Pairing an external RTK GNSS receiver improves precision.

Limits

Good to know

RTK Kit is a layout and grade-checking aid. It is not a survey, it does not set legal property lines, and it does not control equipment. Accuracy depends on your device, sky view, and control. Elevation lookups from public data sources are estimates. Check critical work with a method that fits your job before you cut, fill, or pour.
